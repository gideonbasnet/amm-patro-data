Legal and privacy

Privacy policy

AMM Patro · Last updated: 2026-11-18

AMM Patro is a free Christian calendar app by Apostolic Movement Ministries (AMM). This policy explains what information the app keeps, where, and what it sends.

Who we are

AMM Patro is published by Apostolic Movement Ministries (AMM), the developer responsible for this policy and for the app's handling of data. Questions or requests: Admin@AMMbible.com.

In short

  • No ads, no analytics, no crash reports. Signing in with Google is optional and only backs up your notes to your own Google Drive.
  • Everything you enter stays on your phone, unless you choose that Drive backup. AMM never receives a copy.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

Data collected and shared

AMM collects no data on servers of its own and shares no data with anyone. Only if you choose to sign in with Google: the app reads your Google account's email address (to show which account is used) and sends your notes, sermons and prayers to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, and brings them back from there. Only this app can open that folder; AMM cannot see it. This is what AMM declares in the Data safety section of Google Play. Notes are locked on your phone with your backup password before they leave it, so neither Google nor AMM can read them.

Kept only on your phone

  • Settings: language, country, theme, clock format, notification times and days.
  • Events and their reminders, tithe entries, saved videos.
  • Prayer diary and notes, in an encrypted database whose key is kept in the Android Keystore.
  • Downloaded lists of public holidays and videos.
  • Your backup password, in the Android Keystore; it is never put in a backup or sent anywhere.

Android's own backup to your Google account (end-to-end encrypted with your screen lock) can keep your settings, events and tithe entries for a new phone; notes are not part of it. You can turn it off in the phone's settings.

What comes from the internet

  • GitHub Pages (gideonbasnet.github.io): public holidays of the country you chose and the list of AMM videos. The address contains the country code (for example NP).
  • YouTube (Google): the AMM channel's video list and thumbnails. Settings can limit thumbnails to Wi-Fi. A tapped video opens in the YouTube app.
  • Nepal Rastra Bank (nrb.org.np): the exchange rates, only when you open that screen. Nothing about you is sent.
  • Google (only if you sign in): Google sign-in and Google Drive, to back up and bring back your notes. Google's privacy policy applies.

Like any website, these services can see your IP address and basic technical details; their own privacy policies apply. The app never sends them your name, notes or events.

Permissions

  • Notifications: the morning declaration, today's date, reminders.
  • Exact alarms and starting after a restart: so that notifications arrive on time, even after the phone restarts.
  • Fingerprint or PIN: the notes lock. Android checks it; the app only learns whether it matched, never your fingerprint.

Nothing outside the app

  • The app never reads your contacts, messages, call log, location, photos or files, camera, microphone, clipboard, the list of your apps, or your browsing or app history. It has no permission for any of them.
  • From the phone it uses only the clock and language, whether the internet is on Wi-Fi (for thumbnails) and the country of the mobile network (to suggest your country). They stay on the phone; only the country you chose is part of the holidays address, as above.
  • The phone's maker, model and Android version are not read. In the battery help you choose your phone's brand yourself, and a feedback e-mail contains only the app's version.
  • Other apps cannot reach in. On Android 12 and newer, other apps' floating windows are hidden while the app is open. Notes are marked sensitive: on Android 14 and newer only accessibility tools for people with disabilities (such as TalkBack) can read them, a tap is ignored while another window covers them, and the keyboard is asked not to learn what you write. With the notes lock on, notes never appear in screenshots or in recent apps.

What you send yourself

A backup file you make is locked with your backup password and goes only where you choose. Without the password nobody can open it. If you email us, we use your address only to reply.

A feedback e-mail contains only the app's name and version, and you see it before sending.

Security

  • Every internet connection of the app is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Notes are encrypted on the phone.
  • AMM has no servers that store user data.
  • The Drive backup uses only the app's own hidden folder (the drive.appdata permission), never your other Drive files.
  • Backup files and the Drive backup are locked on your phone, before they leave it, with AES-256 and a key made from your own backup password. AMM and Google cannot open them, and AMM cannot recover a forgotten password.

How long data is kept

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Downloaded holiday and video lists are replaced when newer ones arrive. The Drive backup stays in your Google Drive until you delete it in the app or remove the app's access in your Google account.

Deleting your data

Phone Settings → Apps → AMM Patro → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstalling the app, deletes everything. AMM holds no copy, so there is nothing else to delete.

Drive backup: in the Notes tab, tap your account's letter at the top, then "Delete backup from Drive" and "Sign out". You can also remove AMM Patro's access at myaccount.google.com → Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services.

Children

The app is suitable for everyone. It is not designed specifically for children, and it collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

You have the privacy rights given by the law of the country where you live. Because AMM holds no data about you, there is nothing for us to show, correct or delete; write to us with any question.

Changes

Before the app starts sending or keeping any new data, this policy is updated and the date above changes.
