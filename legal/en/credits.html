Bible texts

The full verse under each daily declaration is copied word for word from these published translations. The app never translates or writes Bible verses.

  • Hindi (IRV): इंडियन रिवाइज्ड वर्जन (IRV) हिंदी - 2019 · © 2017, 2018, 2019 Bridge Connectivity Solutions · CC BY-SA 4.0 · अंत के संदर्भ-पद ( ) हटाए गए · https://ebible.org/hin2017/

Changes made to texts under the CC BY-SA 4.0 licence (creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0) are listed above (cross-references or editorial notes removed); no word of a verse is changed. Where a language has no Bible the app may use, it shows a note in that language instead of the full verse.

Public holidays

Nepal: official notices of the Government of Nepal. Other countries: the open-source holidays library (github.com/vacanza/holidays, MIT licence); some dates are estimates.
